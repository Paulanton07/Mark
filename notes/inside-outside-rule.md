# Inside pieces / outside pieces — Paul's rule

Said in chat 2026-11-01 (Paul Anton), while correcting my X-leg drawing:

> When building there are inside pieces and outside pieces/planks. Remember.
> Inside planks run parallel same direction outside planks for x legs. In opposite direction

> Yes both planks for legs are one on top of other but both are parallel and of equal length.
> Remember there are two x legs, four planks

How I read it, applied to the trough:

- Two X frames, four leg planks. In each frame the two planks lie one on top of the other.
- The two planks in a frame are parallel, same lean, equal length, one plank thickness apart.
- The offset is sideways (out of the frame's plane). Not up. Both feet therefore land on the floor.
- The two frames are a mirror pair: the "outside" plank swaps sides.
- No notch, no packer, no longer cut on one foot.
