## The X joint

- Four leg planks. Two frames.
- In one frame, two planks cross. One lies on top of the other.
- They are parallel. Same lean. Same length.
- The gap between them is one plank thickness, and it is sideways, out of the frame's plane.
- So the top plank does not ride up. Its foot still reaches the floor.
- The second frame is the mirror. The outside plank swaps sides.
- No notch. No packer. No longer cut on one foot.

## Where I was wrong

- I drew the two planks sharing one plane.
- In one plane the top plank has to sit a thickness proud at the foot. That is the only reason the packer idea existed.
- They do not share a plane. That was the whole error.
- So it was a drawing fault, not a build fault. Page 1 of v9 now draws the far plank first, near plank over it, and says the offset is sideways.

## On the bench

- Cut all four legs the same length.
- Stand one frame up. Lay the second plank on it. Screw through the near plank into the far one, one thickness of travel.
- Both feet land level. Check with a straight edge across the pair.
