X-Leg Herb Trough your build · draft v1

  • Your numbers. Two X-leg frames carry a 900 long trough between them.
  • Legs 4×700, one end cut 32°. Cross planks 8×900, four front and four back. Planks 80 wide × 23 thick.
  • The trough sits in the V of the two X frames. Its floor is the first two 900s; the rest stack up the sides.
  • The sides are not cut to a number. They are scribed off an offcut held in the V, one on top of the last.
  • Shown at 900 long, four planks deep. Dimensions in mm.
Stage 1 - the joint that decides the box. Two 700 legs crossed at their centres. The saw cut at the foot and the angle at the crossing set everything above them: get the crossing flush on the clamped plank and the two frames stand square. Confirm the two angles against your build photo.

Material

  • Untreated pine or hardwood. Untreated: it is a food bed.
  • 35 mm part-threaded screws for the crossing and the side courses.
  • A 6 mm or 10 mm bolt through each crossing if you want it to never move.
  • Stainless or coated only. Wood oil (Woodoc) outside and on the feet.
Two rules that decide this build
  • One saw setting for all four feet. Cut every 32° end off the same stop. One foot cut on its own and the box stands on three legs.
  • Lock the crossing. A single screw lets the X scissor. Add 3-4 screws, or bolt it.
Open - to confirm against your build photo

The build in four stages picture sequence

Four stages, in order. Do not start the sides until the walls are screwed down and level.
1
Stage 1 - the X legs. Cut one end of each 700 leg at 32°. Cross two: centre 350 along, 40 across. Screw or bolt the crossing, 22.5°. Make two, mirrored - one plank inside, one outside, left and right.
2
Stage 2 - stand them and start the floor. Stand the two X sets parallel. Cut one 900 at 22.5° to sit snug in the V. The back one lands 23 mm higher, so trim 23 mm off it so both tops come level.
3
Stage 3 - the rest of the 900s. 4 planks on the front, 4 on the back, flush with the left and right X. The back sits 23 mm proud behind the outside leg. Screw down front and back.
4
Stage 4 - the sides. Hold an offcut in the V, mark the angle, cut the first side piece. Next piece higher: mark off the first, cut, glue and screw on top. Third the same. Mirror the other end.

Stage by stage 1 – 2

#StageWhy
1The X legs.
  • Four legs, 700 long. Cut one end of each at 32° - all four off the same saw setting.
  • Find the centre of each leg: 350 along, 40 across (half of 80).
  • Clamp a straight plank to the table. Press the 32° end flush against it.
  • Lay the second leg on top to make the X. Pilot hole at the centre.
  • Drive a 35 mm part-threaded screw so the two pieces sit at 22.5° and the foot sits flush to the clamped plank. About 305 mm between the two top corners.
  • Then 3-4 more screws, or swap to a 6 mm or 10 mm bolt through the crossing.
  • Make the second set a mirror: one plank inside, one outside, on both left and right.
  • One saw setting for all four feet = feet that sit level. Cut them one at a time and they fight.
  • The clamped plank is the reference - it stops the X leaning while you screw.
  • A single screw lets the X scissor. Extra screws or a bolt lock the crossing.
  • Mirrored handed frames make the box symmetrical, so the trough sits level.
2Stand them and lay the first two planks.
  • Stand the two X sets parallel, on the ground.
  • Lay the first two 900 planks in the V, one front, one back.
  • Cut one edge of the front plank at 22.5° so it sits snug in the V.
  • The back plank lands on top of the front, so it sits 23 mm higher. Trim 23 mm off it so both tops come level.
  • Both X sets parallel = the box goes up straight.
  • The 22.5° cut makes the plank nest into the V, not rock on the corner.
  • Level first planks = level box. This is the one that sets it.

Stage by stage 3 – 4

#StageWhy
3Lay the rest of the 900s.
  • Carry on stacking: 4 planks on the front, 4 on the back, one on top of the other.
  • All flush with the left and right X planks.
  • The back stacks 23 mm proud, behind the outside leg.
  • Both sides finish at the same height.
  • Screw the stack down, front and back, into the X frames.
  • Flush to the X = straight walls, no stepping.
  • The back offset clears the leg, so both walls meet the frame the same way.
  • Equal height both sides = a level rim.
4The sides, scribed off an offcut.
  • Hold an offcut horizontal in the bottom of the V. Draw a line from inside to mark the angle of the first side piece.
  • Cut to the line, fit it in the V.
  • Hold a longer offcut above the first, mark off it, cut the second, glue and screw it on top of the first.
  • Third the same, on top of the second.
  • Do the same for the opposite end.
  • The V is not a set angle you can measure off a drawing - you scribe each piece off the one below.
  • Glue and screw: end grain is weak, the glue takes the load, the screws hold it while it sets.
  • Three pieces fill the end from the floor to the rim.
