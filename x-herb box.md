planks are 80mm wide and 23mm thick
4x 700 legs
8x 900 cross pieces four on each side

cut one side of each 4x legs 32degrees
measure find centre of each leg plank, (700 / 2 = 35). 
and 80 \ 2 = 40 this will give you the centre of each leg plank.
lay one leg on the table flush with a straight plank clamped to the table so it doesn't move
press the 32 degree flush to straight plank then place second leg piece on top to create the x.
now you have a small pilot hole drilled into each leg plank in the centre so using the 35mm screw with the shank as explained 35mm so that the screw will not come out the other side, 
you screw into the centre so that the two x pieces are at 22.5 degrees, and bottom angle is sitting flush to the clamped plank. should be about 305mm between the two top corners of each plank. I can send pics for that if need be.
once satisfied that the angle and distance is good and that the bottom angle for legs are flush to the clamped plank proceed to add another 3 or 4 screws and or you can use 6mm or 10mm nuts and bolts instead of screws , generally the maker will use whats available.

remember each set of legs should line up left and right one inside, one plank leg outside same on left and right, mirror image,
the reason is its one plank on top of another 23mm x 2. The 900 cross pieces will go flush to the outside leg on the front of box and on back the 900 will be 23mm slightly proud of back leg , ask if you don't understand this.

next secret :
when I build you start by setting left and right x leg set parallel to each other, lay you first two planks inside the v shape bottom of box,
the trick / secret is to cut one 900 on one edge to 22.5 degreed so it sits snug in the v first plank so its a 80mm plank, sits snug in bottom lying inside ontop of fron x legs plank, now if you do the same for the back side leg plank the second plank will lie ontop of first which makes second plank 23mm higher than first plamk so I had to cut the 23mm off second plank so when the first two 900mm cross planks are sitting same height, then I lay the next 8 planks one ontop of the other, 4 on front side, 4 on back side, all flush with left and right x plank, the back because behind outside leg on the inside has to be 23mm proud . (as mentioned earlier).
so if I stack four 900mm planks one on top of other on front side and 4 900mm cross planks on back side both sides should be the same height, this is why the first two sitting bottom in the v need to start off the same height, 

once the cross pieces are screwed down front and back, left and right its time to cut for sides, 
the method I used was to take an offcut and place or hold it up horizontal against the bottom v starting from bottom hold the offcut and draw a line from inside to mark the angle size of first side piece, cut it along your marked lins and fit into v, next another little longer offcut do same hold above the first side picee and mark with pencil , cut second piece and glue and screw it ontop of first side piece, and finally the third one, mark and cut to size, fit glue and screw ontop of second side. do the same for the opposite side,
